Park Lake Construction and Development is a Central Florida development and construction firm focused on ground-up, small-bay industrial and flex projects.

The firm is led by a Florida-licensed Certified Building Contractor with a background in construction project management and cost estimating, currently overseeing full-cycle project delivery — from design through construction completion — as a Project Manager at a Winter Park-based general contractor. That experience, running projects from the general contractor's side, shapes how Park Lake approaches its own developments: self-performing construction to control cost, schedule, and quality from the ground up.

License: Florida Certified Building Contractor, CBC1270319. Background: construction project management & cost estimating. Focus: ground-up small-bay industrial & flex development. Market: Space Coast & Central Florida.
